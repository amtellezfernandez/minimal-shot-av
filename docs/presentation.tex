\begin{frame}{Main Result}
  \Large
  Collision persists after the two easiest explanations are supplied:

  \vspace{0.7em}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Actor visibility:} world-frame actor completion leaves raw collision unchanged.
    \item \textbf{Learned ranking:} learned-selector-free direct-grid replay improves only two collision clips.
  \end{itemize}

  \vspace{0.7em}
  \normalsize
  The result is a localization result, not a positive safety claim.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Actor Completion Is Not Enough}
  \small
  \begin{tabular}{lrrrrr}
    \toprule
    Policy & Collision & Offroad & Wrong lane & Progress & Dist.-GT \\
    \midrule
    Axis-constrained clamped & 0.600 & 0.207 & 0.241 & 0.170 & 1.54 \\
    World-frame actor completion & 0.600 & 0.310 & 0.172 & 0.184 & 1.42 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}

  \vspace{0.8em}
  Actor completion changes lane, offroad, progress, and tracking behavior, but does not
  reduce raw collision on the matched 30-scene rerun.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Selector Removal Is Not Enough}
  \small
  \begin{tabular}{lrrrr}
    \toprule
    Policy & Collision & Offroad & Wrong lane & Progress \\
    \midrule
    Clamped collision subset & 1.000 & 0.118 & 0.235 & 0.167 \\
    Direct grid, cost-ranked & 0.889 & 0.125 & 0.312 & 0.246 \\
    Direct grid, max-clearance & 0.889 & 0.059 & 0.176 & 0.174 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}

  \vspace{0.8em}
  On the 18 baseline collision clips, bypassing the learned selector improves only two
  paired clips. The residual boundary is not just learned-ranking error.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Takeaway}
  \Large
  The CoRL claim is narrow and auditable:

  \vspace{0.8em}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Bounded candidate interfaces make transfer failures inspectable.
    \item Actor visibility and learned-selector removal are not sufficient.
    \item External diagnostics must report collision, offroad, lane, progress, and tracking separately.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
